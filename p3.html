01 — Philosophy

We don't just build structures; we frame experiences.

Our practice is rooted in the belief that architecture is a living, breathing entity. We explore the friction between heavy materials and the ephemeral nature of light, creating spaces that feel inevitable.

Read our manifesto
01

Material Honesty

We let materials speak for themselves. Concrete, timber, and steel are left exposed, celebrating their structural logic, texture, and imperfections rather than masking them.

02

Spatial Dynamics

Space is not static. We design sequences of compression and expansion, guiding bodies through volumes to create a physical narrative that changes with the movement of the sun.

03

Context & Response

Every site has a memory. We study the soil, the prevailing winds, the neighbouring structures, and the local culture to build extensions of the landscape, not impositions upon it.

02 — The Method

From sketch to site.

A rigorous four-phase methodology that transforms abstract ideas into tangible, buildable realities without losing the original spark.

01

Exploration

Understanding the brief, mapping the site's topography, and establishing a conceptual framework through physical sketching and study models.

ConceptSite AnalysisModel Making
02

Schematic

Translating concepts into spatial arrangements. We test scale, massing, and flow, defining the relationship between interior volumes and exterior envelopes.

MassingCirculationZoning
03

Development

Technical resolution. We detail the joints, select the specific material batches, and coordinate with structural and MEP engineers to ensure design integrity.

Execution
